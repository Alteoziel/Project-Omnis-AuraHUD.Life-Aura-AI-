Mixamo (preferred)
------------------
1. On this PC, open https://www.mixamo.com and sign in with Adobe (browser only — do not paste passwords into chat).
2. Download a humanoid (e.g. Y Bot) T-pose FBX and these clips (FBX for Unity, 30 fps): Idle, Walking, Sitting, Talking.
3. Drop them in Assets/Mixamo/User/ (create the folder).
4. Rebuild the APK. JarvisHumanoidSetup prefers User/ over CC0/.

CC0 fallback
------------
KayKit Adventurers + Character Animations (CC0) live in Assets/Mixamo/CC0/ when Mixamo/User is empty.
