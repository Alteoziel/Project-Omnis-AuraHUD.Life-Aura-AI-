# Holocomm figure models

Four Kenney Mini Characters (CC0 1.0), remapped as original holocomm messengers.

- Source pack: [Kenney Mini Characters](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters)
- Mirror used: [Papyszoo/CC0-Public-Domain-Models](https://github.com/Papyszoo/CC0-Public-Domain-Models)
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Credit is welcome, not required.
- Files: `sage.glb`, `masked-lord.glb`, `hooded-emperor.glb`, `young-knight.glb`

These are generic low-poly humans. They are not Star Wars, Lucasfilm, or Disney likenesses. Holocomm names, lines, accessories, and voices are original. No film audio.
